This repository accompanies my final Master of Research (MRes) project in Structural Biology at Imperial College London, completed in the Manka Group.

It contains the code used to analyse the distribution of dominant and minor prion conformers across cryo-EM fibrils:

* relion-particle-backplotter – back-plots particles assigned to different 3D classes onto their original micrographs to assess conformer co-localisation within individual fibrils.
* fibril-category-counts – quantifies the proportions of dominant-only, minor-only, and mixed fibrils.
* minor-vs-dominant-ratio-in-mixed – calculates the relative proportions of dominant and minor conformers within mixed fibrils.
* length-vs-mixed-status – compares fibril length between mixed and non-mixed fibrils.
* length-vs-mixed-status-violin-plots – generates violin plots visualising the fibril-length distributions of mixed and non-mixed fibrils.
